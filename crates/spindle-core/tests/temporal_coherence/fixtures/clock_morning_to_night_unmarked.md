At 8 a.m. Aldous was already at his desk, the ledger open and the coffee going
cold beside the blotter. He ran a thumb down the column of figures and frowned.
The telephone did not ring once.

The clock read 11 p.m. now, the office dark around him, and he was still in the
same chair with the same column open, no nearer an answer than when he had sat
down.
