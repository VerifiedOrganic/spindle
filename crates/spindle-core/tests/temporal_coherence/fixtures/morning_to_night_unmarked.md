Aldous woke to grey morning light and ate his breakfast at the long table,
bread and cold meat and a cup of watered wine. He turned the ledger toward the
window and ran a thumb down the column of debts, frowning at the figures. The
dogs in the yard barked at nothing. He set the cup down on the wood.

The black water of the harbour lay still under the midnight sky, and he could
not say how he had come to be standing on the quay with the cold biting through
his coat.
