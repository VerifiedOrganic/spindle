Aldous woke to grey morning light and ate his breakfast at the long table,
bread and cold meat and a cup of watered wine. He turned the ledger toward the
window and ran a thumb down the column of debts, frowning at the figures. He set
the cup down on the wood.

Hours later, the black water of the harbour lay still under the midnight sky,
and he stood on the quay with the cold biting through his coat, no nearer an
answer than he had been at the table.
