# Anti-slop evaluation corpus sources and licenses

`evals/anti_slop/corpus.json` contains **160 negative passages** of public-domain human fiction
and **48 positive passages** of synthetic LLM-style slop across the 12 fiction shelves.

## License and provenance

- **Negative corpus (`NEG001`–`NEG160`):** All negative passages are drawn from pre-1929 works
  in the **United States Public Domain** via **Project Gutenberg** (and include short public-domain
  adaptations exercising literal domain phrases such as grain mixes, equal-parts division,
  ordinary `not X, but Y` clauses, and grounded temporal transitions).
- **Positive corpus (`POS001`–`POS048`):** Original synthetic LLM-style examples authored for this
  repository and released under **CC0-1.0**.
